Privacy Policy

The Prepper App · Effective 12 October 2026

This policy explains what The Prepper App (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. The App is operated by Aimentors, the Netherlands ("we", "us"). Contact: info@theprepperapp.com.

1. The short version

  • The App works without an account. On first launch we create an anonymous user ID so your data can be saved.
  • Your questionnaire answers are sent to our servers to generate your plan. They are processed by an AI model for that purpose.
  • If you create an account, we store the email address or the Apple/Google identifier you sign in with.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • You can delete your account and all its data in the App at any time.

3. How we use it

To provide the App and its features, to generate your plan, to process and restore purchases, to send reminders you asked for, to keep the App secure, to fix bugs, to measure and improve the App and its paywall, and to comply with the law.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Performance of our contract with you (providing the App and purchases), our legitimate interests (security, analytics, improvement), your consent (notifications, account creation), and legal obligations.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that process it for us under contract:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, server functions), hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Anthropic (AI model that writes your plan). Your questionnaire answers are sent for this purpose. They are not used to train the model.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) and Superwall (paywall display and analytics).
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (payments, app distribution, Sign in with Apple / Google).
  • Resend (sending sign-in code emails).
  • Expo (app build and push-notification delivery).

Some of these providers are in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA/UK we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or in a merger or sale of the business.

6. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account or an active anonymous profile. Anonymous profiles that have not been used for 24 months may be deleted. When you delete your account, your account and all data linked to it are permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days; backups are overwritten within 90 days. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. The fastest way to delete everything is in the App: Profile → Delete My Account. For anything else, email info@theprepperapp.com. EEA/UK residents can complain to their data-protection authority. California residents have the rights described in the CCPA; we do not sell or share personal information as defined there.

8. Children

The App is not for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers, access is limited by row-level permissions so you can only reach your own data, and paid-feature checks are done on our servers. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you find a problem.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when we do, and material changes are announced in the App.
